## 9. Campaign timeline

**Status.** Checklist. Head revision on main is `carousel_generations`. Implement in the order below. Each box is one commit, and the console still works after that commit. Each box names the tests that land with it. Task 9.10 runs the full suites and `npm run build`.

**Goal.** Pick a campaign and see its posts.

**What the code does today.** A campaign is a definition. `POST /api/v1/campaigns` (`create_campaign`) requires a name, `skeleton_id`, and `carousel_id`. The carousel has to be bound to that skeleton. `campaign_type` `other` is 400 (`CAMPAIGN_TYPE_OTHER_NOT_IMPLEMENTED`). `spotmu` and `spotmu_test` require at least one platform and one Spotmu interest. `spotmu` also requires at least one account (`CAMPAIGN_ACCOUNTS_REQUIRED_DETAIL`). `spotmu_test` may persist an empty `account_ids` list. Platforms are `instagram` or `tiktok`. Slug and hashtag come from the name (`allocate_unique_campaign_slug`, `hashtag_from_campaign_slug`). Status is `ready`. There is no route that attaches an account after create. Start, pause, resume, and finish (`app/campaigns/lifecycle.py`) flip `campaign_status` only. They do not ingest, assemble, or publish.

`POST /api/v1/campaigns/{campaign_id}/run` (`run_campaign`) calls `_replace_campaign_carousels`. That loads `live_slides` sorted by `(position, id)` and calls `assemble_carousels` in `app/campaigns/assemble.py`. The stored rows are `campaign_carousels` and `campaign_carousel_slides`: slide ids and positions, no size cap, no PNG. Run does not read campaign status, instructions, or `campaign_interests`. The ops detail (`CampaignDetailView` in `CampaignsPage.tsx`) shows the hashtag, the skeleton, the carousel, the accounts, the interests, and those run rows under `#campaign-carousels` ("No carousel yet."). `#run-campaign` calls `runCampaign`. The lede says run assembles accepted slides and does not generate text.

Final is a different assembly. `carousel_assemblies` is one row per `(carousel_id, generation_number)`, hung on `generation_id`. `assembled_carousels.status` is only `draft`. `caption` is the carousel name. `cta_url` is null. There is no hashtag column. `assembled_carousel_frames.asset_ref` is `assembled/{assembly_id}/{frame_id}.png` under `ARTIFACT_STORAGE_DIR`. `blocked` is true for `overlap`, `missing_asset`, or `measure_error`, and the PNG is still written. `GET /api/v1/carousels/{carousel_id}/assembly/frames/{frame_id}/file` (`get_assembled_frame_file`) serves that PNG to a signed-in reader. `assemble_carousel_final` keeps the assembly row on a later call, deletes the previous frame files, and replaces the child rows. `CarouselReadResponse.assembly` is the assembly whose `generation_number` equals the carousel's current number. Duplicate (`duplicate_carousel_generation`) does not copy that row, so after duplicate the detail's `assembly` is null until the next Assemble. Older frames stay on disk and stay addressable by their carousel id and frame id.

The old loop is still the rail. Queue (`QueuePage`) lists `artifacts` with `status` `draft` and approves, rejects, edits the caption, and regenerates `carousel_template_v1`. Schedule (`SchedulePage`) turns an `approved` artifact into a `post_jobs` row (`schedule_approved_artifact`) and calls `POST /api/v1/publish/run/{post_job_id}`. Results (`ResultsPage`) shows a cluster median, the agent summary, and the mock stub. `POST /api/v1/generate/carousel` (`generate_carousel_artifact`) still writes an `artifacts` draft from one Spotmu `SourceItem`. The caption is `build_carousel_caption`: the interest title, `DEFAULT_HASHTAGS`, and the interest URL. Those rows have no generation and no `assembled_carousel_id`.

`publish_post_job` resolves `artifacts.slides[].asset_ref` with `resolve_artifact_slide_media_paths` and calls `PublishAdapter.publish`. `MockPublishAdapter` returns `mock-{platform}-{post_job.id}` and `https://example.invalid/{platform}/p/{post_job.id}`. It logs the media count and does not open the network. `AyrsharePublishAdapter.publish` always raises `AyrshareLivePublishNotWiredError`. `PUBLISH_ADAPTER` defaults to `mock`. `accounts` has no token columns. `cadence_cap_per_day` null means no cap. `raise_if_cadence_cap_exceeded` counts `post_jobs` for that account on the UTC day of `scheduled_for` whose status is not `failed`. Meeting the cap is HTTP 409. Failed jobs do not consume a slot.

`metric_snapshots.post_job_id` is not null. `append_metric_snapshot` stores `views`, `likes`, `comments`, `shares`, `captured_at`, and `raw`. A `published` or `failed` post job can move to `measured`. `append_stub_metric_snapshot` writes `views` 100, `likes` 10, `comments` 2, and `shares` 1 when `platform_post_id` starts with `mock-`. Nothing in this tree pulls those counts from Instagram or TikTok.

There is no `campaign_posts` table. An assembled carousel cannot be given a time, and the campaign detail does not list posts.

**Settled for this phase.** The tasks use these. The open decisions at the end are the choices Mu can still change.

### Open decisions

These are the questions section 9 leaves open. The tasks above use the recommended default so a commit can name a file.

1. **What a campaign rule contains.** The scope says campaign rules are not designed in this phase, and it asks what a rule contains, where it lives, and how it picks posts. Recommended default: no rule table and no picker. The operator chooses the assembled carousel, the linked account, and the clock time. The only limit that applies on its own is `accounts.cadence_cap_per_day`, counted across campaigns. Say if a rule should live on the campaign or on the account, or if it should pick the post.
2. **Whether approval is its own status.** The agreed labels are upcoming, posted, and failed. Approval has to become a post status. Recommended default: approval is the act that adds the assembled carousel to the timeline, and the row is stored `upcoming`. There is no `approved` value and no reject value. Removing an upcoming post deletes the row. Say if a post should sit in a fourth status until it has a time.
3. **How `draft`, `approved`, and `scheduled` map.** The migration says those three need a decision before they all become upcoming, and that template-loop artifacts which were never part of a generation stay off the carousel posts. Recommended default: do not copy `artifacts` or `post_jobs` into `campaign_posts`. On the cross-campaign list, a `post_jobs` row is the row that is already a post: `scheduled` and `publishing` read as upcoming, `published` and `measured` read as posted, and `failed` stays failed. A `draft`, `approved`, or `rejected` artifact with no post job stays off the list, so `approved` does not become upcoming. Say if an approved artifact with no time should appear as upcoming.
4. **Who picks the clock time.** The choice is the operator, or a rule inside the account cap. Recommended default: the operator picks `scheduled_for` and presses Publish. The time may be in the past. No worker reads the clock. The cap is the existing per-day number, and the count is non-failed post jobs plus non-failed campaign posts on that UTC day. A null cap stays unlimited. A failed row does not consume a slot. Say if a worker should publish when the time arrives, or if the cap should ignore the old post jobs.
5. **Whether a post can be edited after it has a time.** Recommended default: an upcoming post can change `scheduled_for` or be removed. Caption, link, and frames stay the assembled carousel's and are not edited on the post. A posted or failed post cannot change its time or its content. Failed can be mock-published again. Re-assemble of a generation that has any campaign post is 409 and leaves the PNGs. The next try is Duplicate, then Assemble. Say if an upcoming caption should be editable, or if re-assemble should copy the PNGs onto the post and then replace the assembly.
6. **Whether create still requires platforms and a Spotmu interest.** Mu decided: a Spotmu interest is optional. Platforms stay required. `spotmu` still requires an account, and `spotmu_test` may still omit accounts. A campaign with no linked account cannot take a post. This phase does not add a way to attach an account after create. The timeline does not read the interest for the frame or the caption. The optional interest lands in task 9.1. `campaign_interests` has no `NOT NULL` parent column, so no migration changes `campaigns` for it.
7. **Whether a cross-campaign view remains, and as what.** Recommended default: one list, Posts, in place of Queue, Schedule, and Results. It shows status, time, campaign name, caption, and results. It does not approve, schedule, or publish, and it does not show the cluster median or the agent summary. Those actions for a campaign post live on the campaign. The three old routes stay registered. A post job with no campaign shows `Earlier queue`. Say if the three pages should stay on the rail, or if the list should still mock-publish.
